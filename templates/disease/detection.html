Leaf health check

A photo can help you describe a problem. Image analysis is a starting point, not a confirmed diagnosis.

Count affected plants and record field conditions

Yellowing, spots and wilting have several possible causes. Record how many plants are affected, soil moisture and recent rain to decide what to check next.

Record a field observation

JPG, PNG or WEBP, up to 8 MB. Include both sides of the leaf where possible.

The photo is used for this check. If remote analysis is enabled, it is sent to the image analysis provider. Avoid faces and personal details.

Prepare for expert advice

  • Photograph both sides of the affected leaf and the whole plant in daylight.
  • Record the crop, variety, affected area and when symptoms started.
  • Contact your local KVK or agricultural extension officer before choosing a treatment.
